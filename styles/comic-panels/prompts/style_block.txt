A single panel from an American adventure comic book of the early 1960s, pencilled and then inked by hand with a brush and a dip pen, printed in four-colour process. Inking: bold, confident black brush contours with strong line-weight variation (thick, tapering outlines and thin interior detail lines), and large shapes of solid black for the shadow sides (spotted blacks): the shadow side of the cottage, the rocks and the lighthouse, the hulls of the boats, the gaps between the planks, the underside of the jetty. A few feathered brush strokes and short hatch lines at the shadow edges, no fine cross-hatching. Colouring: flat, solid, unmodulated colour fills from a limited printer's palette, each shape one flat colour: deep ultramarine and violet for the upper sky, a dusty rose band, a hot orange band at the horizon, pale cyan and teal blue for the water, warm grey and ochre for the stones, brick red, olive green, bright yellow for the lit windows and lamps. No gradients, no airbrush, no soft shading, no painterly texture, no paper texture, no halftone dots or screen tones (the dot screen is added later in the printing step). Dramatic but clear composition, bold readable silhouettes, slightly simplified shapes, everything equally sharp. Not a painting, not a watercolour, not a 3D render, not a photograph, not a postcard, not an anime illustration, not a European clear-line album page with thin uniform lines.
