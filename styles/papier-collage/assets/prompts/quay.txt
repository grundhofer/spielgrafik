Handmade cut-paper collage for a 2D side-view adventure game. Every shape is a separate, flat piece of real paper – matte coloured construction paper, brown kraft paper, rough watercolour paper and thin translucent tissue paper – cut out with scissors or torn by hand and glued in overlapping layers. Clearly visible paper grain and fibres on every piece. Scissor-cut edges are crisp and slightly irregular; torn edges show a thin ragged white fibrous rim where the paper core shows. Details are smaller paper pieces glued on top: window frames, planks, stones, stripes, punched paper dots for buttons, rivets and stars. Each glued layer casts a thin soft shadow onto the paper directly beneath it. Bold, simple, slightly naive hand-cut shapes, few pieces, one flat matte colour per piece: no gradients inside a piece, no painted shading, no outlines, no ink or pencil lines, no airbrush, no glossy highlights. Dusk colours: deep indigo, violet and dusty rose papers in the sky, a strip of warm orange paper at the horizon, slate blue and teal papers for the sea, grey and ochre papers for stone, warm brown kraft paper for wood, dark green and red papers for accents; the lights are pieces of bright yellow paper. Not a painting, not a vector illustration, not a 3D render, not a photograph of a real place.
