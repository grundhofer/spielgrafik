Scene illustration for a Japanese adventure game on a 16-bit home computer of the late 1980s and early 1990s, shown in the picture window of the game screen: high-resolution 640x400 pixel art with only 16 colours on screen, chosen from a 4096-colour palette. Crisp, clean anime-style line art: every wall, beam, piece of furniture, rock, post and object has a thin dark outline of one pixel, no anti-aliasing, no blur. Flat cel shading with one light and one shadow tone per surface. All gradients (sky, sea, glow around the lit window) are made only from regular ordered dithering patterns between two neighbouring colours: fine checkerboard raster, 25% and 75% dot grids, horizontal line patterns, never smooth blends and never noise. Calm, precise, slightly melancholic visual-novel mood. Dusk palette with few colours: deep indigo and violet sky with dithered bands, a band of orange and pink light at the horizon, dark blue sea with short horizontal light dashes, cool grey stone, warm brown wood, warm yellow window light. Avoid: painterly digital painting, soft airbrush gradients, photorealism, 3D rendering, a postcard look, modern pixel art with hundreds of colours, lens flare, bloom.

Series rules, identical for every picture of this series. Time of day: dusk, the same evening as the harbour picture of this series; outdoors and in windows the sky is deep indigo and violet with dithered bands, a band of orange and pink light at the horizon and the first stars. Light: indoors the warm yellow light of the lamp, the candle or the fire is the main light and the rest of the room lies in cool blue-violet shade; outdoors the last light comes from the band at the horizon and the shadows are cool blue-violet. Camera: medium-wide view from standing eye level, straight on, no tilt, no fisheye, vertical lines stay vertical; the eye level (outdoors the horizon line) lies at about 45% of the image height from the top. Layout for the picture window of the game screen: the picture is shown inside a framed window that cuts off a thin strip at the top and at the bottom, so keep important objects away from the top and bottom 6% of the picture; the centre and lower centre stay calm and uncluttered (open floor, path or water), because a character portrait will be placed there later.

Location: the lamp room at the top of the white lighthouse of Lornholm, a fictional island in a northern sea, at dusk, seen from inside.
