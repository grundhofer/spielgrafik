Background art for a modern independent 2D side-view adventure platformer in high-resolution "hi-bit" pixel art, made for a game engine with dynamic 2D lighting. The native game screen is 640 pixels wide: the whole picture is built from small crisp square art pixels of one size on one regular grid (each art pixel a small square block of about 2.4 by 2.4 image pixels), as if the game screen were enlarged without smoothing; no anti-aliasing between clusters, no blur, no mixed pixel sizes, no soft airbrushed gradients, no painterly brush texture. Rich but controlled palette of about 80 colours with hue-shifted colour ramps: every material has 4 to 6 shades placed as clean hand-pixelled clusters, shadows shift towards blue-violet, light tones towards warm peach. Careful texture work: individual stones in the cottage wall and the quay slabs, plank lines in the wood, rounded staves and iron hoops on the barrels, cracks and moss on the rocks, all readable as clear pixel clusters. Selective outlines: dark coloured outlines (deep indigo and plum, never pure black) only around the foreground objects, the distant layers have no outlines. Strong atmospheric depth in clear layers: the far hills, the headland and the lighthouse are lighter, bluer and lower in contrast, as if seen through sea haze; the middle distance with the boats is in between; the foreground quay, cottage and props are the most saturated and detailed.

Lighting: soft, even twilight ambient light from the sky, values in the middle range and fully readable (the game engine darkens the scene and adds all light sources itself). Do NOT paint any light sources: no pools of light on the ground, no glow, no halos, no light rays, no cast light from the window or the lantern, no bright reflections in the water. The cottage window and the lantern glass are plain flat warm-yellow shapes; the lighthouse lamp is only a small plain pale-yellow spot without any beam or rays; no smoke from the chimney (lights, the lighthouse beam, reflections and smoke are animated separately in the game). Sky in smooth stepped horizontal bands with sparse ordered dithering between the bands, from deep indigo at the top through violet and dusky rose to a narrow band of orange at the horizon; a few low flat-shaded clouds; a few single-pixel stars in the upper sky. The sea is calm, drawn with horizontal pixel streaks and short light dashes in deep blue and violet that mirror the sky colours.
