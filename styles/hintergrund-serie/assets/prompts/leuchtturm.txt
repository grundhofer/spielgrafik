Technique: loose transparent washes laid with large soft brushes, wet-in-wet blooms and soft bleeding edges in the sky and the water, crisp dried edges where washes overlap, pigment granulation in the darker areas, opaque matte gouache only for a few dark shapes and small light accents, a few dry-brush strokes for stone and wood texture, a faint loose pencil underdrawing that shows through in places, and the texture of the white paper visible everywhere through the paint.
