A background for a 2D narrative game, designed like an Art Deco travel poster of the late 1920s and early 1930s, printed as a colour lithograph from a small set of flat poster inks.

Technique: the scene is reduced to large, simple, geometric shapes with crisp clean hard edges and no outlines. Every area is either one flat ink or a smooth, even airbrushed gradient between two neighbouring inks, like the spray-gun gradients of 1930s posters (sky, sea, the lit and shaded sides of the buildings, the tower of the lighthouse). Forms are stylised and streamlined: long horizontal bands, clean arcs and circles, straight diagonals, stepped terraces. The rocks of the headland are drawn as stepped, faceted geometric blocks with flat light and shadow sides. The low clouds are two or three long, thin, streamlined horizontal bands with rounded ends, in flat violet and coral tones. The calm sea is a stack of long parallel horizontal bands of deep teal and midnight blue, getting lighter towards the horizon. The buildings are clean geometric volumes: the sides facing the sunset in warm coral and cream, the sides in shadow in flat cool violet and blue. A few thin gold-ochre accent lines: the rim of the sun disc, the railing of the lighthouse gallery, the window frames, the lantern frame. Elegant, calm and monumental, with a low viewpoint and a strong silhouette of the lighthouse against the sky.

Palette: only about eight poster inks: midnight blue, deep teal, dusty violet, plum, warm coral orange, pale cream, terracotta red, and gold ochre; the darkest colour is a deep blue-black, never pure black.
