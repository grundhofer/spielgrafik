Style: an expressive oil painting on canvas used as a background for a narrative role-playing game. Painted fast with very large flat brushes and a palette knife: few big strokes, thick impasto with raised paint ridges, every stroke left visible and unblended. Shapes are simplified to large planes, fewer objects and almost no small detail; forms are built from planes of colour, not from outlines; no line art, no flowers, no fine rigging (each mast is a single stroke). Restrained palette of muted, broken colours: grey-green, ochre, dull violet, slate blue and earth brown, mixed wet-in-wet on the canvas, with only a few complementary accents of warm orange and pale yellow at the light sources (lit window, lantern, lamp, a narrow band at the horizon). Not a postcard sunset: an overcast, cool dusk. Mood: melancholic and literary, like a modern expressionist easel painting. Camera: a gently elevated view, as if from a first-floor window, looking slightly down onto the harbour; the quay runs horizontally across the lower third of the picture and its stone surface is wide and clearly readable, so that game characters can walk left and right on it. Composition for a game screen: the right quarter of the picture will later be covered by a dialogue panel, so place the lighthouse on its headland at roughly two thirds of the picture width, not at the edge, and keep the far right edge for sea, rocks and sky. Light effects and smoke are added later as separate animated layers: paint the lighthouse lamp only as a glowing spot of thick yellow paint with no light beam or rays, and above the chimney only a short, faint wisp of smoke. Avoid: flowers, fine rigging, ropes drawn as thin lines, many small props, fine detail, a saturated postcard sunset, smooth digital airbrush gradients, photorealism, clean vector shapes, cartoon outlines, cel shading, a 3D-render look, fine noise, a frame around the painting.

Location: the small harbour of Lornholm, a fictional island in a northern sea, at dusk. A stone quay with a short wooden jetty runs along the lower third of the picture. Two small wooden fishing boats are moored at the jetty. On a rocky headland on the right stands a white lighthouse with one red band, its lamp lit. On the left a small stone harbour-master's cottage with a lit window and a smoking chimney. On the quay: two barrels, a stack of crates, a coil of rope and an iron lantern on a wooden post. Calm sea, low clouds, a band of orange light at the horizon, the first stars.

Leave the quay in the foreground free of characters: it is a game background, characters are added later as separate sprites.
