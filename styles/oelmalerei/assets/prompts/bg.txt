Style: an expressive oil painting on canvas used as a background for a narrative role-playing game. Thick impasto paint: heavy, clearly visible directional brushstrokes and palette-knife marks, raised paint ridges, loose and confident handling; forms are built from bold planes of colour, not from outlines; no line art. Bold, saturated colour with strong temperature contrast: deep ultramarine and prussian-blue shadows, teal and viridian water, warm cadmium orange and vermilion light, ochre, rose and violet accents; dusk painted with complementary colours, never grey or muddy. Every light source (lit window, lamp, horizon) glows with thick strokes of orange and lemon yellow. Mood: melancholic and literary, like a modern expressionist easel painting. Camera: a gently elevated view, as if from a first-floor window, looking slightly down onto the harbour; the quay runs horizontally across the lower third of the picture and its stone surface is wide and clearly readable, so that game characters can walk left and right on it. Composition for a game screen: the right quarter of the picture will later be covered by a dialogue panel, so place the lighthouse on its headland at roughly two thirds of the picture width, not at the edge, and keep the far right edge for sea, rocks and sky. Avoid: smooth digital airbrush gradients, photorealism, clean vector shapes, cartoon outlines, cel shading, a 3D-render look, fine noise, a frame around the painting.

Location: the small harbour of Lornholm, a fictional island in a northern sea, at dusk. A stone quay with a short wooden jetty runs along the lower third of the picture. Two small wooden fishing boats are moored at the jetty. On a rocky headland on the right stands a white lighthouse with one red band, its lamp lit. On the left a small stone harbour-master's cottage with a lit window and a smoking chimney. On the quay: two barrels, a stack of crates, a coil of rope and an iron lantern on a wooden post. Calm sea, low clouds, a band of orange light at the horizon, the first stars.

Leave the quay in the foreground free of characters: it is a game background, characters are added later as separate sprites.
